I am a research intern at [AIGENDRUG](https://aigendrug.com), an AI-driven drug discovery company founded and led by [Sun Kim](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lI_oWS8AAAAJ&hl=en), Professor at Seoul National University. I lead research projects on developing generative models for small molecules and antibodies.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science at KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology), where I gained early research experience in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) at [IC Lab](https://ic.kaist.ac.kr) and in Large Language Models (LLMs) at [Bookend Inc.](https://www.bookend.tech/sentencify), before shifting my focus toward computational biology and drug discovery.

I am currently preparing to pursue a Ph.D. to further develop these research interests, with the goal of advancing generative modeling as a bridge connecting chemistry and biology.

**Research interests:** small-molecule generation, antibody&ndash;antigen binding affinity prediction, single-cell transcriptomics, spatial transcriptomics.
